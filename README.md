========================================
1. OVERALL BRAND DIRECTION
========================================

Create a luxurious, feminine, elegant, modern jewellery shopping website.

The visual direction should feel inspired by premium modern Indian jewellery e-commerce websites such as CaratLane and Swashaa, but DO NOT copy their exact design, code, layout, imagery, branding, or content.

Create an original Twinklingz design system.

Brand personality:
- Elegant
- Premium
- Feminine
- Contemporary
- Sophisticated
- Romantic
- Minimal
- Trustworthy
- Instagram-friendly
- Mobile-first

The website must NOT feel like a generic Shopify template.

It should feel like a professionally designed premium jewellery brand.

========================================
4. OPENING SCROLL VIDEO EXPERIENCE
========================================

This is extremely important.

The homepage should begin with a cinematic FULL-SCREEN jewellery video.

Do NOT simply autoplay a normal video.

Create a scroll-controlled video experience.

The video should occupy approximately the first 100vh visually but use a long scroll section (around 250–400vh) to control its progression.

As the visitor scrolls:
- video progresses smoothly
- video remains sticky/pinned
- elegant text appears/disappears at specific scroll positions
- jewellery details become visible progressively
- subtle zoom/parallax effects may be used
- final frames transition naturally into the normal homepage

Suggested overlay sequence:

FRAME 1:
TWINKLINGZ

FRAME 2:
Jewellery made for your moments.

FRAME 3:
Elegant.
Timeless.
Uniquely You.

FRAME 4:
Because every sparkle tells a story.

FRAME 5:
TWINKLINGZ
by Priya Kaur

CTA:
EXPLORE THE COLLECTION

Text should fade, slide or reveal very subtly.

NO aggressive animations.

Performance:
Use optimized video loading, poster image, lazy loading and graceful fallback for low-performance/mobile devices.

Respect prefers-reduced-motion.
